# Tern identity and provider colors

## Product

**Tern** takes its name from the small seabird: light, observant, and at home near an island. The product phrase is **“A quiet home for your coding agents.”** The folded-wing mark is original vector geometry, shared by the SwiftUI app and icon generator. It does not incorporate provider logos.

The app's theme is independent of provider identity. Theme choices are swatches without color-name labels. Green review indicators are reserved for unreviewed deliveries, not generic success or a live connection.

## Interaction references

The compact top-edge surface and restrained multi-session layout were informed by [Open Island](https://github.com/Octane0411/open-vibe-island) and [Vibe Island](https://vibeisland.app/). Their source, icons, screenshots and fonts are not bundled. Tern's Swift implementation and folded-wing artwork are independent.
